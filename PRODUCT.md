## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML/CSS for the coming-soon prototype (`site/`). No build step. Confirmed by the user in the init interview.

## Users

- **Primary:** video editors who cut short-form video in Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro or CapCut. They want to rebuild a video's edit style quickly.
- **Secondary:** content creators who want a specific video's editing style reproduced without learning to edit it themselves.

## Product Purpose

Editrace reverse-engineers short-form videos into editable templates (cut decisions, pacing, transitions, motion, text and beats), then applies them in the user's editor. The coming-soon page tells visitors what Editrace is and that it is not yet launched. It is a prototype landing page, not the product app.

## Positioning

"Rebuild the edit in minutes." A faithful reconstruction of a video's editing decisions, not an exact copy. Applied directly in the editor through the Editrace Bridge and an MCP integration.

## Operating Context

- Editors evaluate tools by how they fit existing editor workflows (Premiere, Resolve, Final Cut, CapCut), so the page should name those tools.
- Visitors arrive from short-form video and creator communities, not from a sales process.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed: the product is planned, not launched. The coming-soon page does not promise a launch date, pricing or a waitlist.
- Out of scope for this page: auth, the product app and any waitlist backend (follow-up work).
- Undecided: launch date, pricing, waitlist mechanism, and whether the page needs a contact or sign-up form.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Editrace.
- Positioning line: "Rebuild the edit in minutes."
- No visual identity has been confirmed yet. The visual world is for new-work to establish.

## Evidence on Hand

- No product screenshots, customer quotes, case studies or metrics exist yet. Future work must not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Be honest that the product is not yet available.
- Speak to editors in their own tools' terms.
